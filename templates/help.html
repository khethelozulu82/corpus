Help & User Guide

Getting Started

Welcome to the IsiZulu Cultural Corpus! This software helps you explore isiZulu words and phrases with English translations, cultural context, and more.

How to Search

Go to the Search page to find isiZulu, English, Swati or Sotho words and phrases:

  • Search by word or phrase — short queries (under 3 characters) match the whole word, longer queries also match part of a word
  • Search in narrows the fields searched: words & translations, phrases & example sentences, or cultural context
  • Part of speech filters results to nouns, verbs, greetings, proverbs, and so on
  • Exact match only returns words that match your query exactly
  • If nothing matches, you'll see a short list of similarly-spelled suggestions to choose from

Text-to-Speech Feature

Click the 🔊 icon next to any isiZulu word to hear its pronunciation. For best results:

  • Use a modern browser that supports speech synthesis
  • Ensure your volume is turned on
  • isiZulu voice may not be available in all browsers
  • Please note that the pronounciation may not always be accurate

Saving Favourites

Click the ☆ icon next to any word to add it to your favourites. You can:

  • Access your favourite words from the Favourites page
  • Remove words by clicking the star again
  • Use favourites to build your personal vocabulary list

Viewing Trends

The Trends page shows statistics about the corpus:

  • Most frequently searched words
  • Common word pairs
  • Overall corpus statistics

Search History

The History page keeps your 5 most recent distinct searches, newest first. Searching the same term again moves it to the top rather than adding a duplicate. You can remove a single entry or clear your whole history; history is private to your account.

Downloading the Corpus

The Search page has a "Download Database" button that generates a JSON file of the whole corpus. You need to be logged in to download it.

Need More Help?

If you have questions or encounter issues:

  • Check back here for updated guidance
  • Contact the system administrators
  • Consult isiZulu language resources for additional learning
